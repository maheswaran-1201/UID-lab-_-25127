Mata Amritanandamayi Devi (born Sudhamani Idamannel on September 27, 1953), known fondly as Amma ("Mother") or the "hugging saint", is a world-renowned Indian spiritual leader and humanitarian from Kerala. She is globally recognized for embracing millions as a profound act of compassion, offering unconditional love to alleviate physical and emotional suffering.
